Our Mayor

Hon Mayor
Cllr Z.J Sibisi

The Mayor of Alfred Duma Local Municipality is committed to improving the lives of all residents through transparent governance, effective service delivery, and community development. Under the Mayor’s leadership, the municipality prioritizes health, education, safety, infrastructure, and economic growth. The Mayor actively engages with communities, ensuring that citizens’ voices are heard and their needs are addressed.

  • Promoting quality healthcare and education services
  • Supporting local business growth and economic development
  • Ensuring safety, law enforcement, and community well-being
  • Encouraging infrastructure development and urban planning
  • Fostering transparency, accountability, and citizen participation
  • Preserving local culture, heritage, and community values
