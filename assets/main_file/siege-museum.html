The Siege Museum

The Alfred Duma Local Municipality Siege Museum stands as a testament to the rich history of the Ladysmith area during the Anglo-Boer War (1899-1902). Our museum preserves and showcases the artifacts, stories, and heritage of the 118-day siege that shaped our community's identity.


Housed in the historic Town Hall building, the museum features extensive collections including military artifacts, personal diaries, photographs, and interactive exhibits that bring this pivotal period to life. Through our carefully curated displays and educational programs, we aim to honor the past while educating future generations about this significant chapter in South African history.

Museum Highlights

  • Anglo-Boer War Artifacts 500+ Items
  • Interactive Timeline Exhibit Digital Display
  • Personal Diaries & Letters Historical Documents
  • Photographic Archive 1000+ Images
  • Military Uniform Collection Complete Sets
  • Educational Programs School Tours

The Siege Museum is open Tuesday to Sunday from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM, and closed on Mondays and public holidays. Admission fees: Adults R30, Pensioners and Students R15, Children under 12 R10, and School groups (by appointment) R5 per learner. Special rates available for international tourists.

Yes, we offer guided tours conducted by knowledgeable museum staff. Tours are available in English and isiZulu. Standard tours last approximately 45 minutes. For groups larger than 10 people or specialized tours focusing on specific aspects of the siege, we recommend booking in advance by calling +27 (366) 372-231 ext. 245.

Absolutely. We offer comprehensive educational programs aligned with the CAPS curriculum for grades 4-12. Programs include interactive workshops, artifact handling sessions, and guided tours tailored to different age groups. Teachers can download educational resources from our website or contact our education officer to schedule a visit.

We welcome donations of historical items related to the Anglo-Boer War and Ladysmith's history. Please contact our curator at +27 (366) 372-231 ext. 247 or email museum@alfredduma.gov.za to discuss potential donations. Our team will assess the historical significance and relevance to our collection before accepting any items.
