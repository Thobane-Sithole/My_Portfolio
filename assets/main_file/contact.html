Our mission

The Alfred Duma local Municipality is dedicated to building a thriving and inclusive community where every resident has the opportunity to succeed. Create an environment that support economic freedom, encourages entrepreneurship, and empowers people from all walks of life.

Our Vision

By 2041, Alfred Duma Local Municipality is intent on being one of the KwaZulu Natal’s major economic and cultural hubs, with vibrant industrial sector and rich musical heritage

Alfred Duma Local Municipality

Alfred Duma Local Municipality is located in the heart of KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa, covering both urban and rural areas. It is known for its vibrant communities, rich culture, and scenic landscapes connecting key towns and trade routes.
