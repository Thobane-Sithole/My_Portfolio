Our Public Swimming Pools

The Alfred Duma Local Municipality maintains several public swimming pools that provide safe, clean, and affordable recreational opportunities for residents of all ages. Our pools are perfect for cooling off during hot summer days, learning to swim, or getting some exercise.


All our swimming facilities are regularly maintained and supervised by certified lifeguards to ensure everyone's safety. We offer swimming lessons for children and adults, aqua aerobics classes, and special family days throughout the swimming season.

Our Public Swimming Pools

  • Ladysmith Olympic Pool Olympic Size
  • Ezakheni Community Pool Family Friendly
  • White Road town swimming Pool Family size
  • Colenso Swimming Pool Recently Renovated
  • St chad's swimming Pool community Pool
  • Limithill Swimming Pool Splash Pad

Our swimming pools operate from September to April each year. Regular hours are Monday to Friday: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Weekends: 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM. During school holidays, extended hours may apply. Please check with individual facilities for specific schedules.

Admission fees may differ by location and age group. Generally, children under 5 enter for free, children (5-12 years) pay a nominal fee of R10, adults (13-59 years) pay R20, and seniors (60+ years) pay R10. Family packages and group rates are also available. Please contact the specific pool for exact pricing.

Yes, we offer swimming lessons for all ages and skill levels. Our certified instructors provide beginner, intermediate, and advanced classes. Registration opens in August for the summer season. Private lessons are also available upon request.

Yes, you can book our pools for private events after regular operating hours. Please contact our Community Services Department at +27 (366) 372-231 ext. 312 or email communityservices@alfredduma.gov.za for availability and booking procedures.
